# Catalogue integration paused — 0.5.0

At the owner's request, external catalogue and title-artwork integration has been
removed from the launcher for now. There are no TMDB/OMDb/TVmaze requests, title-logo
overlays or API-key setup panels. Titles are text; images and playback links come
from installed apps through Android TV's content provider.

Earlier research remains in ARTWORK-RESEARCH.md for reference. It is not an instruction
to configure or reintroduce a catalogue. The0.4.0 credential files are no longer read;
no saved key is sent anywhere. Source-image resolution and baked-in badges remain
provider limitations.
